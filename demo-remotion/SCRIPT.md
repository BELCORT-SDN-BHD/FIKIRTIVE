### Scene 1 — Hook · the pain  (0:00–0:11)
**VO:** "Every few weeks, a better AI video model ships. And every time you
switch, you lose what made the project yours — your characters, your locations,
your look."
**On screen:** model names flashing (Kling · Veo · Seedance · LTX); a character's
face subtly drifting/changing across them. Dark, tense, fast.

### Scene 2 — Promise  (0:11–0:21)
**VO:** "Artlio is the entity layer for AI video. Lock your characters, places and
products once — reference them anywhere, across every model."
**On screen:** Artlio logotype + tagline, clean reveal.

### Scene 3 — Elements + the @mention wedge  (0:21–0:44)
**VO:** "Start with Elements. Create a character, a location, a product. Add the
reference images you have — or generate a set. Now they're locked, on-model. And
in any prompt, just type @ — your character is there, by name, every time."
**On screen (real app):** Elements grid → "New element" dialog → an entity with
its reference thumbs → cut to a prompt bar, type `@maya`, the @mention dropdown,
the chip resolving in the prompt.

### Scene 4 — Storyboard as segments  (0:44–1:14)
**VO:** "Plan your film as segments. Each one is a start frame and an optional end
frame — the keyframes of a clip. Generate them from your prompt, or upload your
own. Pick your model, your duration, even a fade. Then Animate — image-to-video,
holding the exact identity you locked in."
**On screen (real app):** storyboard board → a dual-frame card (Start → End
slots) → model / seconds / transition selects → Animate → the clip playing.

### Scene 5 — Model-neutral by design  (1:14–1:30)
**VO:** "Artlio is model-neutral by design. The same entities, the same
storyboard — Kling, Veo, Seedance, LTX. Switch models freely; your story stays
consistent."
**On screen:** the model dropdown cycling; the same segment, consistent character.

### Scene 6 — Editor + export  (1:30–1:54)
**VO:** "Assemble in the built-in editor. Pull a finished segment straight from
the storyboard. Trim, reorder, add transitions, balance the audio on every clip.
Then export a finished film — rendered for you, server-side."
**On screen (real app):** editor with timeline + right Inspector (fade / volume)
→ "→ Add to editor" from a storyboard card → Export → render progress → Download
MP4.

### Scene 7 — Close / CTA  (1:54–2:05)
**VO:** "Your entities. Every model. One film. Artlio."
**On screen:** logotype + tagline + URL / CTA. Resolve.
