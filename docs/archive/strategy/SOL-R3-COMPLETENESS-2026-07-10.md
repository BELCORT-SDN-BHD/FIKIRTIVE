# FIKIRTIVE Round 3 — complete product-level omission sweep

**Date:** 2026-07-10  
**Scope:** `docs/BLUEPRINT.md` + `docs/MASTERPLAN.md` + `docs/northstar/PAGE-INVENTORY.md` + `docs/northstar/WAVE-C-ORDER.md` + `docs/strategy/TWO-BRAIN-MEMO-2026-07.md` + R1 (`docs/strategy/SOL-R1-2026-07-10.md`), checked against the current product surface and binding review decisions.

## Verdict

The plan is not yet product-complete enough to take founding-cohort money. It is unusually complete as a **logged-in creation city**, but still incomplete as a **paid merchant service**. The missing product is the system around the factory: a truthful commercial front door, qualification and activation, authority and off-app approvals, connector health, external-effect reconciliation, receipts, merchant billing documents, support and operator rescue, platform-standing safety, data/rights controls, and renewal/offboarding.

The central planning defect is also unresolved: the adjudicated July direction says to light one complete mandate-to-receipt loop first, with every link best-in-class, while the execution runner still orders factory breadth first (`docs/MASTERPLAN.md:41-48`, `docs/MASTERPLAN.md:129-182`; `docs/strategy/TWO-BRAIN-MEMO-2026-07.md:7-25`; `docs/research/GRILL-VERDICTS-2026-07-03.md:227`). Until the runner is rewritten, the product can keep accumulating beautiful rooms without proving that one merchant job completes safely in the real world.

### Severity and placement key

- **Launch-blocker:** must be implemented, or explicitly excluded in the signed cohort scope, before accepting money.
- **Launch-blocker\*:** scope-specific—persona, channel, language, market, billing model, or optional capability; either support it end-to-end or exclude it explicitly from the founding cohort.
- **Pre-scale:** a disclosed manual/concierge path is acceptable for a small cohort, but the state and operator work must still be recorded.
- **Later:** deliberately postpone without making the paid founding loop dishonest or unsafe.
- **Constitution†:** only through the founder's §7 amendment process; this report does not propose editing `docs/BLUEPRINT.md` directly.
- A finding is not automatically a new page. Several belong inside the existing Notifications, Connections, Billing, Account, onboarding, Campaign, and admin surfaces.

## 11. Do not re-litigate these settled decisions

- **Marketing email remains separate from transactional approval/exception egress.** The product can defer campaigns by email while still requiring reliable service messages.
- **Made-with stays later.** Withhold the design-preview footer from production now; decide its merchant policy before treating it as a growth loop.
- **White-label remains prohibited.** Partner-assisted onboarding must remain visibly FIKIRTIVE and merchant-controlled (`docs/BLUEPRINT.md:63`).
- **Internal exactly-once credit handling is not the gap.** The gap is merchant-visible payment exceptions and uncertain external effects; do not weaken the existing money gate (`docs/BLUEPRINT.md:49-52`).
- **Nightly backup/PITR is already underway.** The gaps are merchant-level undo, selective restoration, retention, and deletion propagation (`docs/MASTERPLAN.md:68`).
- **Accessibility is covered.** Preserve and test the existing design contract (`docs/design-system/design-rules.md:514-557`).

## 12. Ten fixes before taking any founding-cohort money, ranked

1. **Replace the current runner with one named, bounded real loop and a signed cohort cell** (`P01`, `P05`, `P08`). Pick one persona, one market, one offer type, one acquisition path, one external action, one outcome, one support promise, and explicit exclusions. Put a benchmark and fallback on every link.
2. **Implement the minimal mandate-to-receipt spine** (`P02`, `P03`, `P04`, `P06`, `X05`). Minimum objects: Mandate, WorkUnit, AuthorityGrant, ActionAttempt, Exception, BusinessEvent/Receipt, evidence grade, provenance, correction, and operator intervention.
3. **Publish capability truth and remove every unsupported claim before acquisition** (`L01`, `G01`, `I01`). Public home/pricing must say exactly what each connected account can read, draft, remind, publish, message, measure, and reconcile; fix the current Instagram/Facebook publishing claim.
4. **Ship the complete first-merchant lifecycle through first value** (`L02`–`L06`, `L11`, `X01`, `X02`). Readiness gate, validated import or disclosed concierge equivalent, shadow rehearsal, first live approval, mobile resume, first receipt, stuck recovery, human case, and measurable time-to-value must work as one path.
5. **Make the paid contract operational, including merchant bookkeeping** (`O01`–`O04`, `L08`, `L09`, `G03`). Lock pricing/entitlements, failed-payment and refund behavior, credit expiry/rollover, cancellation effects, FIKIRTIVE invoices/receipts/credit notes, and the applicable MY e-Invoice operating path.
6. **Deliver approve-anywhere as a secure service, not an in-app badge** (`N01`–`N08`, `M02`). Verified off-app egress, version-bound decision card, step-up, outbox, delivery/bounce/independent fallback, deadline/reminder/backup escalation, route consent/fee rules, race safety, anti-phishing, and receipt/correction notifications are one launch feature.
7. **Build connector health and a safe manual fallback for the chosen cohort channel** (`I02`–`I05`, `I09`–`I11`). Include permission drift, token expiry, account restriction, quota/outage, freshness, reconnect/backlog review, custodian transfer, and—if WhatsApp is in scope—number/WABA/BSP migration and template/quality/window states.
8. **Build external-effect certainty and one exception/reconciliation center** (`O05`, `I06`–`I08`, `I12`, `O14`). Add webhook inbox/replay, provider IDs, `MAYBE_APPLIED`, pull reconciliation, partial multi-target receipts, channel-fee variance/disputes where applicable, safe retry/compensation, merchant decisions, and operator reason/minutes.
9. **Install platform-standing and rights gates before generating or publishing real merchant content** (`O08`, `R01`–`R06`, `K09`, `X03`). Merchant eligibility, claim evidence, consent/suppression, likeness/music/output rights, provenance, country×vertical×channel rules, takedown/appeal, and portfolio kill switches are launch infrastructure.
10. **Complete the minimum paid-cohort trust floor** (`O06`, `O09`–`O13`). Provide the cohort handbook and tracked case route, authenticated manual data-rights path, retention/deletion rules, confirmation/correction boundaries, MFA or step-up plus session recovery, and a written incident/breach notification runbook. Self-service privacy, full recycle bin, public status, and productized incident objects remain pre-scale.

**Money gate:** do not interpret “founding cohort” as permission to leave these manual but invisible. A manual workaround is acceptable only where marked pre-scale, is disclosed to the merchant, produces the same state/evidence as the future automated path, records operator minutes, and has a named failure owner.
