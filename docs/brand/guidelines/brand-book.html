The marks

The F mark represents the company: hand-drawn strokes with rounded terminals, ending in three dots. The Otto cloud represents Otto, Fikirtive’s AI marketing operator.

01 · Logos

Clear space and minimum size

x/4 x/4 x/4 x/4
x = the mark’s height. Nothing enters the clear zone — type, marks, or the page edge.
Clear space
One quarter of the mark height (x/4), on all four sides.
Minimum size
Tile 16 px / 4 mm. Bare F mark 24 px / 6 mm. Lockup 24 px / 6 mm tile height.
Scaling
Scale proportionally. Use the official exports; do not redraw.
Placement
Browser tabs and app icons: coral tile. Formal documents: black tile. On color or photography: paper or ink mark, inside a tile.

05 · Audience

Audience

Small-business owners.

They run the store and the marketing alone — no team and no time to learn prompting. They need plans they can read, costs shown before anything runs, and a record of everything that happened.

Global by default. Southeast Asia is the first market, not the frame of reference. Anything involving money is specific: amounts, counts, dates.

The test for every line of copy: readable in one pass.

07 · Grammar and style

Grammar and style

Spelling
American English: color, favorite, organize.
Casing
Sentence case everywhere — titles, buttons, navigation. All caps only for small labels, with letter-spacing.
Oxford comma
Always: posts, images, and videos.
Currency
RM 2,350.00 — currency prefix, thousands separator, two decimals. Credits as 1,240 cr.
Dates
12 Aug 2026. Month and year: Aug 2026. Short contexts: Fri, 12 Aug.
Numbers
Numerals in UI copy: 4 images, 2 done.
Product terms
Fikirtive in text. fikirtive as the wordmark. Otto always capitalized; credits and canvas always lowercase.
Never
In customer-facing copy: “Coming soon,” AI vendor or model names, numbers without a source.
